# atscvdetector policy draft handover

Prepared 16 October 2026. On request, the Terms, Privacy and Refund pages now display an effective date of 16 October 2026 instead of draft labels. This editorial approval is not a certification of compliance. No separate Legal page was added. No policy can prevent litigation or override mandatory rights.

## Outstanding operational verification

- Confirm the exact contracting entity behind Code Dev Solutions, its status, service address and any disclosures required for online trading. No company number or address has been invented.
- Confirm control of atscvdetector.com and that support@atscvdetector.com receives messages. The domain is branding only at present; local APP_URL was preserved so local routes and OAuth are not broken.
- Identify production hosting, server countries, email provider, all subprocessors, backup cycle and specific retention periods. Implement and verify cleanup rather than relying on a policy statement. Check log contents and remove unnecessary personal information.
- Confirm the external AI account's billing/data-use configuration. The reviewed provider terms distinguish paid and unpaid processing, and prohibit submitting personal or confidential information to unpaid services. Do not enable real-person CV processing on an unsuitable plan. The public draft uses a generic external-AI description as requested; final collection-time disclosure must identify the provider, purposes, transfers and applicable data use. A policy does not fix an unsuitable processor arrangement.
- Assess Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act and SI 155/2024 for controller licensing, data protection officer requirements, lawful grounds, security, breach handling and cross-border transfers. Do not claim registration, certification or universal GDPR compliance without verification.
- Confirm operational capacity for the proposed acknowledgement/review targets, deletion requests and the adult-only account terms. Existing account creation does not verify age. Google-only account deletion currently needs password reset or support; do not promise one-click deletion for all accounts.
- Refund proposal: 14-calendar-day voluntary review window, five-business-day acknowledgement target, fourteen-business-day decision/update target and ten-business-day initiation target after approval. These do not shorten statutory rights. Paid checkout remains disabled. Revisit actual price, currency, taxes, billing/cancellation, delivery and processor disclosures before activating purchases.
- Have a Zimbabwe-qualified lawyer review the drafts and current operating practices before public reliance. The wording preserves mandatory remedies and does not impose a blanket no-refund or no-liability rule.
