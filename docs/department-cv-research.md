# Department CV structure research

Reviewed 19 October 2026. These are practical defaults, not mandatory professional or legal requirements. A department contains many occupations; a single representative example cannot describe every role. Role selection sets the headline only. Gallery examples are fictional and are never saved into the user's CV. Existing user sections, custom headings and saved placement take priority.

## Shared document structure

One reading column; selectable text; contact details in the document body; familiar summary, experience, skills, education and qualification headings; role/company/dates and bullet descriptions. No photographs, skill ratings or decorative sidebars. The same renderer supplies editing, review and export. Short examples use the page height without inflating text; longer content can grow. This improves readability and parsing, and is not a guarantee of a particular ATS score.

CareerOneStop's [resume overview and ATS guidance](https://cloudfront.careeronestop.org/Veterans/JobSearch/ResumesAndApplications/resume-overview.aspx), [work experience guidance](https://cloudfront.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Resumes/ResumeGuide/work-experience.aspx) and [education guidance](https://cloudfront.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Resumes/ResumeGuide/education.aspx) inform the common structure. These templates default to experience before education; applicants can move education earlier when their career stage makes that appropriate.

## Department defaults and evidence

All begin with identity/contact information and Professional Summary. The following order is our implementation choice based on the sources, rather than a claim that an institution prescribes that exact sequence. Optional user content is retained after the default sections unless the user has placed it elsewhere.

| Department | Default main sections | Content focus and research |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accounting and Finance | Experience; Accounting Skills; Professional Qualifications & Training; Education; Financial Achievements | Financial records, reconciliation, reporting and audit support. No default Projects. [O*NET Accountants and Auditors](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/13-2011.00) and [ACCA qualification routes](https://www.accaglobal.com/gb/en/qualifications/accountancy-career/working.html). Qualifications belong only when actually held or accurately identified as in progress. |
| Admin | Experience; Administrative Skills; Education; Training; Administrative Achievements | Calendar coordination, correspondence and records. [O*NET Administrative Assistants](https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/43-6014.00). |
| Engineering | Technical Skills; Experience; optional Selected Engineering Projects; Education; Certifications & Professional Development; Engineering Achievements | Technical methods, verification, drawings and outcomes. [Harvard engineering resume example](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/harvard-college-resume-example-engineering/). Registration is not prefilled or assumed. |
| Human Resource | Experience; HR Skills; Education; HR Qualifications & Professional Development; HR Achievements | Recruitment, onboarding, HR records and confidentiality. [CIPD Profession Map](https://www.cipd.org/en/the-people-profession/the-profession-map/explore-the-profession-map/), [CIPD membership routes](https://www.cipd.org/uk/membership/routes-to-membership/) and [O*NET HR Specialists](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/13-1071.00). Membership and qualifications are distinct and must be accurately described. |
| Information technology | Technical Skills; Experience; optional Selected Technical Projects; IT Certifications & Training; Education; Technical Achievements | Relevant technologies and evidence of technical work. The representative example is broad technical support, not a claim that all IT roles are developers. [O*NET Software Developers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/15-1252.00) and Harvard's [technology resume resources](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/channels/create-a-resume-cv-or-cover-letter/). |
| Legal and Compliance | Experience; Legal & Compliance Skills; Education; Qualifications & Professional Development; Legal & Compliance Achievements | Research, document review, deadlines and confidentiality. [Yale Law resume advice](https://law.yale.edu/student-life/career-development/alumni/toolkit-alumni-job-seekers/resume-advice). Bar admission applies only to roles requiring it, not every compliance worker; no admission is fabricated. |
| Marketing and Sale | Experience; Marketing & Sales Achievements; Skills; Education; Certifications & Training | Campaign performance, customer relationships, CRM and sales pipeline. [O*NET Marketing Managers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/11-2021.00). No default software Projects. |
| Operation and Production | Experience; Operations & Production Skills; Process Improvement Achievements; Safety, Quality & Technical Training; Education | Output, scheduling, quality, safety and operating records. [O*NET Industrial Production Managers](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/11-3051.00). Training names are not presented as statutory licences. |
| Public relations | Experience; Communication & Campaign Achievements; PR & Communication Skills; Education; Training | Messaging, media relations, coverage and stakeholder communication. [PRSA resume guide](https://jobs.prsa.org/career-resources/find-the-job-1/public-relations-resume-examples-a-complete-guide-for-2026-423). Applicants may use the existing website field for a portfolio. |
| Supply Chain and Logistics | Experience; Supply Chain & Logistics Skills; Supply Chain Achievements; Certifications & Training; Education | Inventory, suppliers, deliveries and systems. [ASCM resume guide](https://jobs.ascm.org/career-resources/on-the-job-3/supply-chain-resume-guide-2026-83) and [ASCM certificate programmes](https://www.ascm.org/learning-development/certificate-programs/). Do not imply a certification without actual attainment. |
| Managers | Experience; Leadership & Business Achievements; Leadership & Management Skills; Education; Management Qualifications & Training | People, resources, budgets and accountable results. [PMI resume guidance](https://www.pmi.org/blog/how-to-build-a-resume-that-stands-out-for-project-roles) informs evidence of scope and outcomes, but its project-specific advice is not imposed on every manager. |

Projects are optional examples for engineering/IT, not mandatory saved fields. A real accounting transformation project or other relevant custom section remains allowed: the change removes irrelevant defaults, not legitimate user evidence. Headings remain editable. All eleven departments remain alphabetical with Managers last.
